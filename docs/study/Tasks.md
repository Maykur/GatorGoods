# Task List

This document is for the participant-facing task list only.

Do not add moderator instructions to this document.

## Study Assumption

For all tasks, assume you are a UF student living on or very near campus. Assume all exchanges should be practical for an on-campus lifestyle.

## Condition Set

Each participant completes all 4 conditions:

1. Buy on GatorGoods
2. Buy on Facebook Marketplace
3. Sell on GatorGoods
4. Sell on Facebook Marketplace

The moderator assigns the order.

## Buyer Tasks

Complete these tasks in both buyer conditions.

For the buyer tasks, assume you want to buy a `mini fridge` that makes sense for a UF student living on or near campus.

### Task B1: Inspect A Mini Fridge Listing

Find one `mini fridge` listing you would seriously consider buying, then open it and look through the available photos, price, item details, and location information.

### Task B2: Evaluate The Seller

Based only on what the platform shows you, decide whether you would trust this seller enough to continue.

### Task B3: Show The First Purchase Step

Go to the point where you would take the first real step toward buying that mini fridge.

Examples:

- opening the offer flow
- opening the message flow
- reaching the point where you could send a first message

You do not need to send a real message to a stranger on Facebook Marketplace.

### Task B4: Evaluate Exchange Coordination

Based on what the platform shows you, explain whether you feel ready to coordinate an on-campus meetup, payment method, and pickup plan.

### Task B5: Make A Final Buying Decision

Decide whether you would continue with this mini-fridge purchase on this platform.

## Seller Tasks

Complete these tasks in both seller conditions.

For the seller tasks, assume you are listing the same study item on both platforms: a `desk lamp`.

Use the provided study image of the desk lamp.

Assume the lamp is `used but in good working condition`.

You may choose your own title, description, and other listing details.

Choose a reasonable price for a student marketplace.

### Task S1: Start A Listing

Use the provided study image of the desk lamp to go through the seller-side listing flow for the assigned platform.

Enter enough information that another student could understand what is being sold.

### Task S2: Review The Seller Experience

Look at the seller-side tools the platform gives you after or during listing creation.

Focus on:

- what information the platform asks you to provide
- how much control you feel you have
- how easy it seems to manage buyer interest

### Task S3: Explain How You Would Handle Buyer Interest

Based on what the platform shows you, explain how you would handle incoming buyer interest or questions on this platform.

### Task S4: Make A Final Selling Decision

Decide whether you would feel comfortable using this platform to sell this item to another student living on or near campus.

## Completion Criteria

A buyer condition is complete when the participant has:

- inspected a mini-fridge listing they would seriously consider
- made a trust judgment
- reached the first meaningful action point
- commented on exchange coordination
- made a final yes/no decision

A seller condition is complete when the participant has:

- gone through the seller-side listing flow
- reviewed the seller-side experience
- explained how they would handle buyer interest
- made a final yes/no decision

## Notes

- Participants do not need to wait for live replies.
- Participants do not need to send real Facebook messages to strangers.
- Participants should stay focused on what the platform itself makes easy or difficult.
