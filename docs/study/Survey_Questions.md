# Survey Questions

This document is formatted for easy copy/paste into Google Docs or Qualtrics.

Use 3 short surveys.

The surveys are split this way so the moderator does not need to stop after every single condition.

---

# Survey 1: Consent and Pre-Study Questions

Give this survey before any tasks begin.

## Consent Reminder

For each statement below, use:

Response format: `Yes / No`

1. I understand that I am participating in a class project evaluation.
2. I understand that my responses will be used for a class report.
3. I understand that I can stop at any time.

## Background Questions

1. How often do you use online marketplaces?

Response format: `Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Very often`

2. Have you ever used Facebook Marketplace before?

Response format: `Yes / No`

3. Have you ever bought or sold something while living on or near a college campus?

Response format: `Yes / No`

4. How comfortable are you with buying from another student online?

Response format: `1 to 7 scale`

5. How comfortable are you with selling to another student online?

Response format: `1 to 7 scale`

## Scenario Check

1. For today’s study, I understand that I should assume I am a UF student living on or very near campus.

Response format: `Yes / No`

# Survey 2: Buyer Comparison Survey

Give this survey after both buyer conditions are complete.

Use the platform names directly in the survey.

For all rating questions below, use:

Response format: `1 to 7 scale from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree`

## Part A: GatorGoods Buyer Questions

1. I had enough information to judge whether the seller seemed trustworthy.
2. I understood what to do if I wanted to continue the purchase.
3. I felt comfortable with the idea of coordinating an on-campus meetup through this platform.
4. This platform felt designed for a student like me.
5. I would continue with this purchase on this platform.

## Part B: Facebook Marketplace Buyer Questions

1. I had enough information to judge whether the seller seemed trustworthy.
2. I understood what to do if I wanted to continue the purchase.
3. I felt comfortable with the idea of coordinating an on-campus meetup through this platform.
4. This platform felt designed for a student like me.
5. I would continue with this purchase on this platform.

## Part C: Buyer Comparison Questions

For each question below, use:

Response format: `GatorGoods / Facebook Marketplace / About the same`

1. Which platform made you feel more confident as a buyer?
2. Which platform made seller trust easier to judge?
3. Which platform made the next step clearer?
4. Which platform felt better suited for an on-campus purchase?

## Part D: Buyer Open-Ended Questions

For each question below, use:

Response format: `Short answer`

1. What most increased your trust on GatorGoods?
2. What most increased or reduced your trust on Facebook Marketplace?
3. What felt most confusing during the buyer tasks?

# Survey 3: Seller and Final Survey

Give this survey after both seller conditions are complete.

For all rating questions below, use:

Response format: `1 to 7 scale from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree`

## Part A: GatorGoods Seller Questions

1. It was clear what information I should provide as a seller.
2. I felt confident that I could create a useful listing on this platform.
3. I felt the platform gave me enough structure to manage buyer interest.
4. This platform felt practical for selling to another student near campus.
5. I would use this platform to sell this item.

## Part B: Facebook Marketplace Seller Questions

1. It was clear what information I should provide as a seller.
2. I felt confident that I could create a useful listing on this platform.
3. I felt the platform gave me enough structure to manage buyer interest.
4. This platform felt practical for selling to another student near campus.
5. I would use this platform to sell this item.

## Part C: Seller Comparison Questions

For each question below, use:

Response format: `GatorGoods / Facebook Marketplace / About the same`

1. Which platform felt easier to use as a seller?
2. Which platform gave you more confidence in handling buyer interest?
3. Which platform felt more structured?
4. Which platform felt better for student-to-student selling?

## Part D: Final Overall Questions

For each question below, use:

Response format: `GatorGoods / Facebook Marketplace / About the same`

1. Overall, which platform would you rather use to buy from?
2. Overall, which platform would you rather use to sell on?
3. Overall, which platform felt more trustworthy?
4. Overall, which platform felt better suited for campus life?

## Part E: Final Open-Ended Questions

For each question below, use:

Response format: `Short answer`

1. What was the single best part of GatorGoods?
2. What was the single best part of Facebook Marketplace?
3. What was the single biggest weakness of GatorGoods?
4. What was the single biggest weakness of Facebook Marketplace?
5. If you had to recommend one platform to another UF student, which would you recommend and why?
