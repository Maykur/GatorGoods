# Primary And Secondary Hypotheses

This study compares GatorGoods against Facebook Marketplace for a simple campus-marketplace use case.

The study is intentionally lightweight and class-appropriate. The goal is to make the comparison clear, not to overcomplicate the analysis.

## Independent Variable

The main independent variable is:

- `Platform`: GatorGoods or Facebook Marketplace

## Main Outcome Areas

The study focuses on these outcome areas:

- trust in the other party
- confidence in completing a campus transaction
- clarity of next steps
- ease of using the platform for buying
- ease of using the platform for selling

## Primary Hypotheses

### H1

Participants will report higher trust and higher willingness to continue a purchase on GatorGoods than on Facebook Marketplace.

### H0_1

There will be no meaningful difference between GatorGoods and Facebook Marketplace in reported trust or willingness to continue a purchase.

### H2

Participants will report that GatorGoods makes campus transaction coordination feel clearer and easier than Facebook Marketplace.

### H0_2

There will be no meaningful difference between GatorGoods and Facebook Marketplace in perceived coordination clarity or ease.

## Secondary Hypotheses

### H3

Participants will report that GatorGoods gives them more useful seller-side structure than Facebook Marketplace.

### H0_3

There will be no meaningful difference between GatorGoods and Facebook Marketplace in perceived seller-side structure.

### H4

Participants will report that GatorGoods feels more appropriate for a student living on or near campus.

### H0_4

There will be no meaningful difference between GatorGoods and Facebook Marketplace in perceived campus fit.

## What To Measure

The moderator should collect both observation notes and survey data.

### Primary Measures

- final buy decision on each platform
- trust rating for each platform
- coordination clarity rating for each platform
- willingness to continue the transaction

### Secondary Measures

- seller-side ease rating
- clarity of listing creation
- confidence in handling buyer interest
- overall platform preference

### Moderator Observation Measures

- condition completion
- time on task for each condition
- visible confusion or hesitation
- comments about trust
- comments about campus practicality

## Simple Analysis Plan

Keep the analysis simple.

Use:

- average ratings by platform
- count of how many participants preferred each platform
- short summary of recurring comments
- simple timing comparison if the times are clean enough

For this class project, descriptive comparison is enough.

If the team wants one sentence for the report:

"We compared GatorGoods and Facebook Marketplace on buying and selling tasks for an on-campus student scenario, then summarized ratings, decisions, and comments across participants."
